02 — The Room

All day,
every day.

Chacha Restaurant is the all-day table on Civil Hospital Road. Mornings start with paratha and chai, afternoons run on karahi and biryani, and the coals stay lit into the late night for kebabs.

Families come for the long table — a place to sit together, eat together, and stay a while. There is outdoor seating for the cooler evenings, and delivery for the nights you would rather eat at home.

  • Breakfast — paratha, halwa puri, chai
  • Lunch & dinner — karahi, BBQ, biryani
  • Late night — kebabs off the coals
  • Seating — family dining, outdoor seating
  • Takeaway — dine-in, takeaway and delivery

04 — From the Karahi

The karahi
is the house.

Tomatoes, green chillies, ginger and ghee, cooked hard and fast in the wok and finished with fresh coriander. It comes to the table still bubbling — that is the point.

Chicken karahi for the everyday table, mutton karahi for the occasion. Either way, order the naan first and let it arrive hot.

Order a karahi — 0300 1881111

05 — From the Coals

Smoke is
the seasoning.

Seekh kebab, tikka and malai boti go over charcoal, not gas. The coals are lit from the afternoon and stay hot into the late night — that is why the evening crowd comes.

06 — Rice & Biryani

The pot
does the talking.

Biryani and pulao are the centre of the family table — a handi in the middle, plates passed around, second helpings expected. Chicken biryani for the regulars, beef pulao when the whole family is in.

It is served with raita and salad, and it is the dish most tables order alongside the karahi.

07 — The Morning Table

Breakfast
is not a side dish.

Chacha opens for breakfast, and Bahawalpur notices. Paratha off the tawa with white butter, halwa puri on the weekends' table, and doodh patti that arrives too hot to hold.

Come early, come hungry, and leave the rest of the day to sort itself out.

08 — Naan & the Tandoor

Hot bread,
every order.

Naan comes out of the tandoor to order — never reheated, never waiting. Tandoori naan for the karahi, roghni naan for the kebabs, and a stack on the table before the food arrives.

09 — Chai & the Late-Night Table

Chai keeps
the lights on.

The last order of the night is usually chai. Clay cups, ginger, and a table that nobody is in a hurry to leave. The outdoor seats fill up as the day cools down, and the garden stays open late.

That is the rhythm of the place: breakfast at dawn, kebabs at dusk, chai in between and after.

10 — The Room

Room for
the whole family.

Big tables, room to spread out, and no rush to turn the table over. That is what a family restaurant is for — the birthday dinners, the after-work karahi, the cousins who all order something different.

For a large gathering, call ahead and the table will be ready when you arrive.

Book a table — 0300 1881111
